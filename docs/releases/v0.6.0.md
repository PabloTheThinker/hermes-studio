## v0.6.0 — the timeline editor, finished enough to cut with

This is the release that turns Hermes Studio from a clipper into an editor. The whole video-editor line — the timeline, the engine that records every change, the tools an AI agent drives over MCP — is merged, tested and shipped in one build. Everything runs on your machine; nothing is uploaded or posted.

### A real timeline editor

Open **Edit** and you get a non-destructive timeline where you and your agent edit the same project:

- **Cutting:** trim, split, lift, ripple delete, move and nudge; **roll, slip and slide**; multi-select by marquee or shift-click, with group move and delete.
- **Markers:** colour, rename and jump between them.
- **In/Out range and snapping** for frame-accurate work.
- **Speed:** constant 0.1–10×, **speed ramps** (graph UI), **reverse** and **freeze frame** — sound follows picture, and one time map drives preview, split and render so they always agree.
- **Titles:** lower third, title and end-card objects in five house fonts, size 50–200%, a dark panel, drag-to-place in the viewer (snaps home, Alt frees), and fade / slide motion (Alt+T). One canvas PNG per title, drawn by the Design renderer so preview and export match pixel-for-pixel.
- **Audio:** a track mixer (mute / solo / gain) with meters and waveforms, fades, volume keys, loudness normalisation, **pan** (track balance) and **ducking** music under voice. After a render you get a measured **loudness readout** (−14 LUFS ±1) taken off the exported file, and a hand-set mix is respected rather than re-levelled.

### A bin and three-point editing

Import or upload into a **media bin**, open a **source viewer** with its own In/Out, then insert (ripple), overwrite or append with the **W / D / E** keys — Final-Cut style. Every edit is one undo step, and a whole cut can be built from the keyboard.

### The engine, and an agent that edits alongside you

Underneath, every change is recorded in an op-log that replays deterministically, so undo/redo, history and conflict handling are exact. The same project is editable three ways — the desk, the command line, and **MCP tools** an AI agent calls — reading and writing the same files. New this release: a project store with a cross-process lock, a timeline engine over HTTP, and the `/mcp` timeline toolset.

### Already in the app

The **Clips** pipeline (long video or a YouTube/Twitch link in, captioned 9:16 shorts out, fully offline) and the **Design** page (carousels, covers, thumbnails; fonts, looks, background removal) are unchanged and still here.

### Under the hood

- The 50-plus sprint branches from the editor build are consolidated into one clean line on `main`.
- The desktop build is assembled and tested on clean machines (a fresh Ubuntu container and a Windows VM) before it is published, and every asset is checksum-verified.
- Bundled FFmpeg, PyAV and OpenCV notices and GPL/LGPL texts ship inside the app, with matching source attached to the release.

### Install or update

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PabloTheThinker/hermes-studio/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
```

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PabloTheThinker/hermes-studio/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

**Or download the installer yourself:** `Hermes-Studio-Setup-0.6.0.exe` (Windows) or `Hermes-Studio-0.6.0.AppImage` (Linux). Check either one against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Linux: `… | bash -s -- --version v0.6.0` pins this release; `--uninstall` removes the app and keeps your clips. It installs to `~/.local/share/hermes-studio` and needs no FUSE.

It runs on your computer only. The speech model (about 75 MB) downloads the first time you clip; after that it works offline. Nothing is uploaded or posted.
