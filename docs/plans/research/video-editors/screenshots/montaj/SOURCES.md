# Screenshot sources: montaj

All files are real. Nothing was generated or edited. Collected Oct 1, 2026 (ET).

- **00:** Pablo's own capture, copied byte-for-byte.
- **01–15:** official images downloaded with curl from montaj.ag and from the official GitHub repo.
- **16–27:** our own full-viewport captures of public montaj.ag pages, taken with Playwright and headless Chrome at 1440×900 with no sign-in and no waitlist entry.

**Important caveat on 16–27.** The "app windows" on montaj.ag are stylised HTML/CSS marketing mock-ups built into the page. They are not screenshots of the shipping Mac app. They show the intended layout and copy, not proof of exact pixels. We found no real screenshot of the app UI anywhere public (the repo README has only the logo, and its `docs/` folder has no images).
