# Edit page spec (Phase 1 UI, Glyph §6 of PLAN-MERGED)

Status: **draft, built on `feat/editor-ui-edit` (stacked on S8). Not ruled.** Comp: `comps/COMP-GLYPH-EDIT-SIDEBAR.png`.

## What it is

A new **Edit** page in the desk (rail: Clips · **Edit** · Design), in `hermes_studio/ui/edit.js` (+ `ui/sw.js`). Every change is a tool call on `POST /mcp` with the person's `ui` token, so the page, Hermes and any MCP client share one engine, one log, one undo and the same cards.

- **Projects:** list (`project_list`) and **New project** (`project_new`: 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5; 30 fps).
- **Left panel:** *Media* (import a local file with optional Whisper words; per-media state and progress from `media.*` events; **Add to end** puts the whole file at the end of V1; **Add as music** puts it on the music track at 25% volume), *Item* (the selected item: a clip's volume 0–200%, speed ½×–2× (the source end is shortened by a few ticks when needed so the length stays whole), fades, and **Crossfade into next** (0.5 s, pulling the following V1 clips in by the overlap, one entry); a text item's text, style and fades) *Scenes* (shot changes from the `scenes` import stage, with thumbnails; click to seek; **Split at every shot change** as one entry) and *Transcript* (words on the timeline, fillers in ember; click to seek, shift-click to select; **Remove fillers**, **Tighten pauses**, **Cut selection**, each previewed first with its cut count and seconds, then one undoable entry).
- **Centre:** the preview. With H.264 (Chrome, Edge, the desktop app) a sequence player over the S4 proxies; otherwise cached engine frames (S5), about 6 a second while playing. Text items are drawn over live video (engine frames already carry them). Word captions are drawn the way the render burns them (`captions.build_ass`): lines of the chosen style's length, broken at a pause over 0.55 s or a sentence end, the word being said in the style's highlight, scaled from a 1920 px frame; the styles come from the desk's `/api/caption-styles` (`captions.preview_styles`), and **no captions** hides them. Engine frames carry no captions, so this shows in both preview modes. Sound follows the render's mix: the V1 video plays at its clip's volume and fades, and every other non-text track (music, voice) plays on a hidden `<audio>` over its S4 proxy, re-seeked only on a new clip or more than 0.25 s of drift so V1 cuts don't make the music skip. The page can't play above 100%, so louder clips play at full volume. It needs AAC (the desktop app, Chrome, Edge); the headless Chromium in `check-edit-page.js` has none, so the e2e checks the plan (`HSEdit._audioPlan(t)`), not the sound.
- **Bottom:** the timeline (ruler, T/V/A tracks, items placed by the engine's resolve rules, playhead). Click selects, drag moves (`move_clip`, snapped to frames; with **snap** ticked an edge within 8 px of another item's start or end, a marker or the playhead lands on it and a dashed amber line shows where; Shift drags freely, and markers snap the same way), **drag an edge to trim** (`trim_clip`: a clip's right edge sets `src_out`, with ripple when ticked; its left edge sets `src_in` and keeps the right edge; a text item's edges set `dur` / `at`), double-click a text item to edit it (`edit_text`), **Split** (`split_clip` at the playhead), **Delete** (with ripple), **+ Text**. Clips show their first thumbnail and their waveform (S4 files). Agent items have an ember outline and a tag (`S · step 3`); a person's last edit an ink outline.
- **Right sidebar (Hermes):** status dot, **follow** (on by default: the playhead jumps to each agent change; a click on the ruler turns it off), the **Ask / Propose / Auto** switch (`set_mode`), one **waiting card** per parked agent edit (**Apply / Skip / Apply the rest**), and one **card per history entry** (who, summary, `v12 → v13`, **Before / after** frames from `history_frames`, **Undo**). Undone entries are dimmed.
- **Top:** project id and version, **Undo / Redo** (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z: linear, whoever made the edit), **Render MP4** (S6) with live progress and a download link; beside it the captions (a style, or **no captions**: `captions: false`) and the size (**full**, or **half**: even width and height, at least 16).
- **Live:** the page reads the project's SSE stream (`fetch`, with `Last-Event-ID` on reconnect): `op.*` reloads the doc, `approval.*` the cards, `media.*` / `render.*` the progress, `mode.changed` the switch.
- **Presets** (toolbar menu, `apply_preset`: one entry and one undo each): Title card and End card (ask for the text), Fade every clip, Crossfade every cut, Duck the music, Close the gaps (V1 clips close up from 0; crossfades and anchored items kept; other tracks stay).
- **Markers:** on the ruler, in amber. **+ Marker** or M puts one at the playhead (`add_marker`); a click selects it and seeks there; drag moves it, double-click renames it, and the Item tab edits its name and time (all `edit_marker`: one entry, one undo); Delete removes it (`remove_marker`).
- **Keys** (the **Keys** button or ? lists them): Space play/pause; ← → one frame, Shift ← → one second; [ ] previous / next edit point (item starts and ends, markers); Home / End; Alt ← → nudges the selected item or marker a frame (Alt+Shift: ten), one entry each; S split; M marker; Delete delete; Ctrl+C copies the selected clip or text item (only when nothing on the page is text-selected), Ctrl+V pastes it at the playhead on its track, Ctrl+D duplicates it right after itself (`insert_clip` / `add_text`; an overlap is refused by the engine and shown); Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z.
- **Slip and roll** (clip Item tab, −1 s / −1 fr / +1 fr / +1 s): **Slip** shows another part of the source in the same place (`slip_clip`; also , and . on the selected clip, Shift for a second, or Alt-drag the clip); **Roll the cut into <next>** moves the cut between two touching or crossfaded clips without changing the total length (`roll_edit`; also Alt-drag the right edge). One entry each.
- **Selection:** a click selects one item or marker; Ctrl/Cmd- or Shift-click adds or removes one; Ctrl+A takes every clip and text item; Esc clears. With several selected, the Item tab lists them, **Delete** removes them all as one entry (markers, then crossfades, then items last-first so a ripple never moves one still to go), and Alt+arrows nudge them together as one entry (the earliest stops at 0; items that ride on a clip are refused). A drag still moves one item.
- **Navigation:** **Fit** or \\ zooms so the whole edit fits; Ctrl+wheel zooms around the pointer (the second under it stays under it); while playing, the view scrolls to keep the playhead in sight.
- **One entry per held key:** repeats of one key action (a held Alt+arrow nudge, , or . slip) add up on screen and land as one entry 350 ms after the keys stop; another action lands the pending one first.
- **Writes queue:** the page's writes, undo and redo run one at a time, each on the version the last one left, so quick actions don't conflict with each other.
- **Drags hold redraws:** a reload that lands mid-drag (an agent edit, or the echo of our own write arriving before its reply) waits until the pointer lets go, so the dragged node never disappears under it. A reload cancels any reload an event queued, since it reads the latest doc (our own write's echo no longer redraws twice).

## The token (S3 §13.3)

- **Desktop:** Electron main makes the token (`crypto.randomBytes(32)`), gives it to the engine in its environment (`HERMES_STUDIO_UI_TOKEN`, removed from the engine's environment at once) and to the desk page over IPC (`window.studio.uiToken()`, answered only for the desk's own origin). Memory only.
- **Browser:** `hermes-studio studio` prints an **Edit page code**; the page asks for it once and keeps it in memory.
- **Media requests** (`<video>`, `<img>`, downloads) can't send a header, so `ui/sw.js` (a service worker) adds `Authorization` to same-origin GETs of project media, frames and render files only. It holds the token in memory.

## Engine additions for the page

- `project_list` (works closed) and `project_new` (app running, `write`) tools; `GET/POST /api/projects`.
- `Tokens.adopt` for a token made by the app; `http_engine.start` adopts `HERMES_STUDIO_UI_TOKEN` or mints one.
- `GET /edit/edit.js`, `GET /sw.js`.
- `tools/list`: 26 tools.

## Checks

- `tests/test_edit_page.py` (12): token adoption (and refusal of a bad one), project tools over /mcp and REST, scopes, closed app, the page files and the rail entry.
- `scripts/check-edit-page.js` (manual, Playwright + Chromium): new project → import → a words file for m1 → add to end → the preview captions "hello world this" with "world" in pop's highlight → an agent adds a title over stdio MCP in Propose mode → the waiting card → Apply → seek and play → before/after frames → drag the clip's right edge 2 s shorter → double-click the title and edit it → a second clip, **Crossfade into next**, 2× speed on it; then keys and markers (Home, Shift+→, →, ], M, Alt+→ nudge, five quick Alt+→ as one entry, Fit, rename, drag 2 s, Keys list, Delete, Ctrl+Z) → roll the cut 1 s through the crossfade and slip the first clip a frame with . → drag the marker to 5 px from the playhead and see it snap → Ctrl+D a clip, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V the title → add the clip as music and check the audio plan at 1 s → Ctrl-click two clips and Delete them as one entry, undo, nudge the two text items together, Ctrl+A, Esc → render at half size without captions and wait for the download link; screenshots 1–9; fails on page errors, a playhead that doesn't move, a wrong trim, a wrong step, nudge or marker drag.
